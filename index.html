Hermes Drive

A private automation assistant for event-management paperwork.

Hermes Drive is a single-user application. It is operated by one person, on their own private server, to reduce the manual work involved in producing recurring event-management documents — agendas, quotations, sponsorship price lists, invitations and delegation letters.

What it does

  • Reads approved document templates and business rules from a private repository.
  • Reads reference data — event names, sponsor names, speaker records — from the owner's own Google Sheets.
  • Generates the finished documents and files them in the correct folder of the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Checks each generated document before it is delivered: page count, layout, stamp and signature.

Who can use it

Nobody but its owner. The application is not offered as a service, has no sign-up, no users other than the account that owns it, and no public interface. These pages exist only because Google requires a published home page and privacy policy for any application that requests access to a Google account.

Privacy: the application accesses only the owner's own Google account, and the data it reads is never sold, shared, or sent to any third party.
